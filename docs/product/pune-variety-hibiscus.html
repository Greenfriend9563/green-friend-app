About Pune Variety Hibiscus

The scientific name for this plant is Hibiscus rosa-sinensis, commonly referred to as a Tropical Hibiscus or Chinese Hibiscus. In local nurseries, variants like this are often called hybrid or exotic hibiscus, especially recognized for their vibrant, multi-colored blooms. This specific flower features a striking color gradient, with a deep maroon center that transitions into a bright magenta ring, extending to soft, creamy-yellow edges, all surrounding a prominent yellow stamen.
